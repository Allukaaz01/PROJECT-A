About us

Valorant is a tactical shooter game developed by Riot Games. It features a unique blend of strategic gameplay and character-based abilities, making it a popular choice among competitive players.

  • Developed by Riot Games
  • Released in June 2020
  • Genre: Tactical Shooter
  • Available on Windows and macOS

For more information, visit the official Valorant website.
